Historical Results ⓘ How these odds are estimated:
The percentages shown for each year are based on simulated lotteries using that year’s historical application data. For example, value of 10% means the choice won 100 times out of 1,000 simulated lotteries for that year.
The smaller date shown is the comparable date used for that year (the date that matches the day of week, month, and week in the month of your choice).
